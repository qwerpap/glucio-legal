Common questions

  • mg/dL or mmol/L? Settings → Units & range. Your readings are converted, nothing is lost.
  • Readings from my sensor? If its app saves glucose to Apple Health, turn on Apple Health in Glucio Settings.
  • Restore my subscription — Settings → Restore purchases, or the Restore link on the subscription screen.
  • Cancel — Settings on your iPhone → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Glucio.
