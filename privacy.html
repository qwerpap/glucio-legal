Privacy Policy

Glucio: Blood Sugar Tracker · Last updated September 27, 2026

Glucio is a personal glucose diary. Your readings, meals, medicines and notes stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no server that stores your health data, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • Glucose readings you type in or import, with their time, context and notes.
  • Meals, medicines and doses you log.
  • Your settings: units, target range, reminders and answers from the first-run questions.
  • Doctor reports (PDF) you create. They leave the app only when you share them yourself.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Glucio reads blood glucose samples from Apple Health to fill in your diary. It never writes to Health. Health data is never sent to us or to any third party, is not used for advertising or marketing, and is not stored in iCloud by Glucio.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Glucio receives only the status of your subscription, never your payment details. We use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier and purchase status to validate subscriptions and measure how the paywall performs; it never receives your readings or any health data.

Notifications

Reading reminders and the reminder a day before a free trial ends are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

Analytics and tracking

Glucio does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers and contains no third-party analytics beyond the subscription service above.

Deleting your data

Remove any entry with the ✕ next to it, or delete Glucio from your iPhone to remove everything it stored. Data in Apple Health is managed in the Health app.

Not medical advice

Glucio is a diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give dosing advice. Estimates such as A1c (GMI) come from your own log and are not lab results.

Children

Glucio is not directed at children under 13.
