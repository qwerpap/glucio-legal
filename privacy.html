Privacy

Updated October 6, 2026

In one line: your glucose diary is kept on your iPhone, Glucio asks for no login, and the only outside service it talks to is the one that manages subscriptions.
Readings, meals, doses, notes

What you type or import, with times, tags and units, plus your target range, reminder times and first-run answers, is stored in Glucio’s own storage on the iPhone. We never receive it and there is no Glucio server that holds health data.

Apple Health

If you switch it on, Glucio asks iOS for permission to read blood glucose samples and fills your diary with them. It does not write to Health. Health data is not sent to us or to anyone else, is not used for advertising or marketing, and stays out of any analytics.

Doctor reports

The PDF is built on the iPhone from the period you pick. It goes only where you send it with the iOS share sheet.

Reminders

Reading reminders, and the reminder the day before a free trial ends, are local notifications scheduled by iOS on your phone. There is no push server.

Subscriptions

Apple takes the payment and we never see card details. The subscription service Adapty receives an anonymous install ID, your purchase status and basic paywall events so it can unlock Pro and restore purchases. It does not receive your readings.

Phone backups

Because the diary lives in the app’s storage, iOS may include it in your iCloud or computer backup of the iPhone if you have those backups on. That is an iOS setting you control; we do not run it.

What is not collected

No advertising ID, no tracking across other apps or sites, no third-party analytics kits besides the subscription service above.

Deleting

Remove an entry with the ✕ beside it, or delete Glucio to remove everything it holds. Samples in Apple Health are managed in the Health app.

Not medical advice

Glucio is a diary. It does not diagnose, treat or advise on doses. An estimated GMI is worked out from your own log and is not a lab result.

Children, changes, contact

Not meant for children under 13. A revised page gets a new date. Questions: netkaktakto@gmail.com.
